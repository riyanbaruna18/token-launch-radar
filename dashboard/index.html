How the radar scores a protocol

Every number below comes from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot of 2026-11-14. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row, so running the program on a future snapshot finds future launches early.

The formula

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains
multiple = median mcap/TVL of the snapshot's live-token protocols
           in the category, shrunk toward the global median
growth   = 1 + 0.6 × tanh(change_7d clamped to −80…+300, scaled by 60)
maturity = 0.55 + 0.45 × rise × decay
           rise climbs to 1.0 over the first 730 days,
           decay falls to 0.30 over the next 1,460 days
chains   = 1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 5)

Why these choices

  • Market multiple, not a flat 0.25. A lending protocol and a DEX do not launch at the same multiple of TVL. The market's own live protocols price each category; the model reads that price from the snapshot.
  • Growth is bounded. A +26% week lifts a protocol about 25%; a −70% week halves it; a +4,448% outlier is capped, not worshipped.
  • Maturity is a hump. Protocols in their prime launch window — one to two years old, no token yet — score highest. A protocol five years old with no token has likely chosen not to launch one.
  • Wrapped, staked and bridged products are out. The board applies the snapshot's own watchlist definition to every row: WBTC, staked ETH and bridges can never launch a token of their own, so they never rank.
  • Confidence is data completeness. A row loses points when the snapshot gives it no 7-day change, no listing date, no category or no chain.

Reading a farm plan

Each plan is generated from the protocol's own record — its category, its chains, its TVL and its age. Capital follows TVL ($50–$250 a position), the hold follows age (30–60 days), and gas follows the chain. Never pay to claim an airdrop: check the protocol's own points page from the link it publishes.
